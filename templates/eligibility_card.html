User Eligibility

This section explains the requirements and legal eligibility to use the ShramikConnect platform as a worker or employer.

Last Updated: March 2026

Eligibility Requirements

This page explains the eligibility criteria required to use the ShramikConnect platform. These requirements ensure that both workers and employers can use the platform safely and responsibly.

1.

Minimum Age Requirement

To use the ShramikConnect platform, users must be at least 18 years old.

Individuals below the age of 18 are not permitted to create accounts or use the platform.

If ShramikConnect discovers that an account belongs to a minor, the account may be suspended or permanently removed.

This rule ensures that users are legally capable of communicating and entering work agreements.

2.

Geographic Availability

ShramikConnect is designed primarily for users located within India.

Workers and employers using the platform should be located in India or offering job opportunities within Indian locations.

The platform may restrict certain services if the user is located outside supported regions.

3.

Worker Eligibility

To register as a worker on ShramikConnect, users must meet the following requirements:

  • Must have a valid mobile number for account verification
  • Must provide basic personal information
  • Must provide skill details or work category
  • Must provide accurate location information

Workers are responsible for ensuring that their profile details are correct and updated.

ShramikConnect may request identity verification if suspicious activity or complaints are reported.

4.

Employer Eligibility

Employers on ShramikConnect may be individual persons or organizations seeking workers for different types of jobs.

To use the platform as an employer:

  • A valid mobile number verification is required
  • Employers must provide accurate job information
  • Employers must provide the correct job location

Employers are not required to have a business registration or GST registration to post jobs on the platform.

5.

Accurate Information Requirement

All users must provide true, accurate, and complete information when creating accounts or posting job details.

Providing false or misleading information may result in actions such as:

  • Removal of job listings
  • Temporary account suspension
  • Permanent account termination in serious cases

This rule helps maintain a trustworthy environment for all users.

6.

Account Responsibility

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their account information.

Users must not:

  • Share their account credentials with others
  • Allow unauthorized access to their account
  • Use another person's account to access the platform

Any activity performed through a user account will be considered the responsibility of the account holder.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect acts only as a digital platform that connects workers and employers.

The platform:

  • Does not act as an employer
  • Does not guarantee job availability
  • Does not manage or process payments between users

Workers and employers communicate directly and are responsible for negotiating job details and payment terms independently.
