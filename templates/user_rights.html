User Rights

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

As a user of ShramikConnect, you have certain rights regarding your personal information. This section explains the rights available to you and how you can exercise them. We are committed to respecting your privacy and providing you with control over your data.

1.

Right to Access

You have the right to request a copy of the personal information we hold about you. This includes:

  • Account information (name, mobile number, email)
  • Profile details (location, skills, work experience)
  • Job application history and interactions
  • Ratings and feedback received

To request access to your data, please contact us at support@shramikconnect.in. We will respond to your request within 15 working days as required by law.

2.

Right to Rectification

If you believe that any personal information we hold about you is inaccurate or incomplete, you have the right to request correction.

You can update certain information directly through your account settings, including:

  • Name and contact details
  • Skills and work preferences
  • Location information

For corrections to other information, please contact our support team.

3.

Right to Deletion (Right to be Forgotten)

You have the right to request deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances, such as:

  • Your data is no longer needed for the purposes for which it was collected
  • You withdraw your consent (where processing is based on consent)
  • Your data has been processed unlawfully

Account Deletion Process

To delete your account, please send an email to support@shramikconnect.in with your request. We will process your account deletion within 2 days of receiving your request. Once deleted, your account and associated data will be permanently removed from our active systems.

Some information may be retained longer if required for legal compliance or to resolve disputes, but only to the extent necessary.

4.

Right to Restrict Processing

You have the right to request that we restrict the processing of your personal information in certain situations, such as:

  • You contest the accuracy of your data (while we verify it)
  • The processing is unlawful, but you oppose deletion
  • We no longer need the data, but you require it for legal claims

If processing is restricted, we will only store your data and not use it further without your consent or for legal purposes.

5.

Right to Data Portability

You have the right to receive your personal information in a structured, commonly used, and machine-readable format. This allows you to transfer your data to another service provider if you wish.

To request a copy of your data in portable format, please contact us at support@shramikconnect.in. We will provide your data in a standard format such as JSON or CSV.

6.

Right to Object

You have the right to object to the processing of your personal information in certain circumstances, including:

  • Processing based on legitimate interests (unless we have compelling legitimate grounds)
  • Processing for direct marketing purposes (though we do not currently engage in marketing)

If you object, we will stop processing your data unless we have legitimate grounds that override your interests or for legal claims.

7.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of your rights described above, please contact us using the following methods:

Email

support@shramikconnect.in

Response Time

We will respond to your request within 15 working days as required by applicable law.

To help us process your request efficiently, please provide as much detail as possible about the specific right you wish to exercise and the information it relates to. We may need to verify your identity before processing your request.

Important Notice

Your privacy rights are important to us. ShramikConnect is committed to respecting your choices and providing you with control over your personal information.

If you have any questions about your rights or need assistance exercising them, please contact us at support@shramikconnect.in. We're here to help.
