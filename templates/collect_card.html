Information We Collect

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains the types of personal information we collect from users of the ShramikConnect platform. We collect only the information necessary to provide our services and maintain a safe, trustworthy environment for workers and employers.

1.

Account Information

When you create an account on ShramikConnect, we collect basic information required for registration:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number (verified via OTP)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Password (encrypted)

We do not allow users under 18 years of age to create accounts on our platform.

2.

Profile Information

To help workers and employers connect effectively, we collect additional profile details:

  • Location (city/area)
  • Skills and work category (for workers)
  • Work experience (optional)
  • Ratings and feedback from employers/workers

This information helps us match workers with relevant job opportunities and build trust within the community.

3.

Identity Verification Documents

In certain cases, we may request identity verification documents to enhance trust and safety on the platform:

  • Aadhar card (optional but recommended for verification)
  • PAN card (optional alternative)

Secure Storage

When provided, identity documents are stored securely in our database and used only for verification purposes. We take appropriate measures to protect this sensitive information.

Providing identity documents is voluntary but may help build trust with employers and increase your chances of getting hired.

4.

Technical and Usage Data

When you use our platform, we automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Device information (browser type, operating system)
  • IP address
  • Usage patterns (pages visited, time spent)
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

This data helps us improve our services, fix technical issues, and enhance user experience.

5.

Hosting and Infrastructure

ShramikConnect is hosted on Render, a cloud platform that provides reliable and secure hosting services. Your data is stored on Render's secure servers located in accordance with industry standards.

Render implements security measures to protect hosted data, and we choose trusted infrastructure partners to ensure your information remains safe.

For more information about Render's security practices, you can refer to their security documentation.

6.

Information from Third Parties

We may receive information about you from third parties in limited circumstances:

  • YouTube videos embedded on our platform (standard YouTube API)
  • Information shared by other users about you (e.g., feedback, ratings)

Embedded YouTube videos may collect data according to Google's privacy policy. We do not control how YouTube collects or uses this information.

7.

Children's Data

ShramikConnect is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18.

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a user under 18, we will take steps to delete that information.

8.

International Users

Currently, ShramikConnect services are available only to users located in India. We do not intentionally collect data from international users.

If this changes in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect collects only the information necessary to provide our services and maintain a safe platform for workers and employers.

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Any sensitive data (such as identity documents) is stored securely and used only for verification purposes.
