Account Termination Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains the conditions under which a user account on the ShramikConnect platform may be suspended, restricted, or permanently terminated. ShramikConnect aims to maintain a safe and trustworthy platform for workers and employers. Accounts that violate platform policies may be subject to appropriate action.

1.

Reasons for Account Termination

ShramikConnect may suspend or terminate user accounts in situations such as:

  • Repeated violation of platform policies
  • Fraudulent activity or scams
  • Providing false or misleading information
  • Harassment or abusive behavior toward other users
  • Posting illegal or prohibited job requests

The platform reserves the right to take action when necessary to protect the community.

2.

Temporary Account Suspension

In certain cases, accounts may be temporarily suspended instead of permanently terminated.

Temporary suspension may occur when:

  • Minor policy violations are reported
  • Suspicious activity is detected
  • Platform review is required

During suspension, users may not be able to access certain platform features.

3.

Permanent Account Termination

Accounts may be permanently terminated if serious violations occur.

Examples include:

  • Fraud or scams
  • Repeated abusive behavior
  • Illegal activities through the platform
  • Severe violations of platform conduct rules

Once an account is permanently terminated, access to the platform may be restricted.

4.

User Requested Account Deletion

Users may request the deletion of their own account if they no longer wish to use the platform.

Upon request:

  • The account may be deactivated
  • Certain personal information may be removed from active systems

However, some information may be retained if required for legal or security purposes.

5.

Platform Rights

ShramikConnect reserves the right to:

  • Investigate suspicious activities
  • Restrict access to certain features
  • Remove accounts that threaten platform safety

These actions help maintain a secure and reliable environment for all users.

6.

Effect of Termination

If an account is terminated:

  • Access to the platform may be removed
  • User profiles may become inactive
  • Ongoing communication between users may be interrupted

Users should ensure they follow platform policies to avoid termination.

7.

Appeals and Review

If a user believes their account was terminated incorrectly, they may contact platform support for review.

Requests for review may include:

  • Explanation of the situation
  • Supporting information or clarification

ShramikConnect may review such requests but does not guarantee account restoration.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect enforces termination policies to maintain a safe, fair, and respectful environment for all users.

Users are encouraged to follow platform rules and communicate responsibly to avoid account restrictions.
