Location Data

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

Location information helps workers and employers connect based on geographic proximity. This section explains what location data we collect, how we use it, and your choices regarding location information on the ShramikConnect platform.

1.

Types of Location Data We Collect

ShramikConnect collects different types of location information depending on how you use the platform:

City/Area Level Location

  • Required: When you register, we ask for your city or area. This helps match workers with jobs in their vicinity and helps employers find workers near their job sites.

Precise Location (Optional)

  • Optional: Workers may choose to share their precise location (GPS coordinates) to help employers find them more accurately. This is completely optional and can be controlled through your device settings.

You are in full control of your location information. Precise location sharing is never required to use ShramikConnect.

2.

How We Use Location Data

We use location information to provide and improve our services:

  • Job Matching: Show workers job opportunities near their location
  • Worker Discovery: Help employers find workers in their area
  • Search Filters: Allow users to filter jobs or workers by distance or location
  • Platform Improvement: Analyze aggregate location data to understand where our platform is being used and improve service coverage

Privacy First

We never share your precise location with other users without your consent. When workers choose to share precise location, it is only visible to employers they interact with, and only for the purpose of facilitating work arrangements.

3.

Location Data in Job Postings

When employers post jobs, they provide location information about where the work will take place:

  • Job Location: Employers specify the city/area where work is needed. This helps workers find jobs near them.
  • Work Site Details: Additional location details may be shared with workers after they are hired, through direct communication.

Job location information is visible to all workers on the platform to help them decide whether to apply.

4.

Your Choices and Control

You have several ways to control your location information:

Profile Settings

You can update your city/area information anytime through your profile settings.

Device Permissions

If you've enabled precise location, you can revoke this permission through your device settings at any time.

Location History

We do not track or store your location history. Only your current city/area (or current precise location if you choose to share it) is used.

Tip: Providing your accurate city/area helps you find more relevant job opportunities. You don't need to share your exact address - your city or area is sufficient for most job matches.

5.

Data Retention and Deletion

We handle location data according to the following practices:

  • City/Area: Your city/area information is stored as part of your profile and remains until you update it or delete your account.
  • Precise Location: If you choose to share precise location, it is used only during your current session and not stored permanently.
  • Account Deletion: When you delete your account (within 2 days of request), all location information associated with your profile is removed from our active systems.

Some location data may be retained in anonymized form for analytical purposes, but this cannot be linked back to you.

Important Notice

Your location privacy matters to us. ShramikConnect is designed to work effectively with just your city or area information. Precise location sharing is completely optional and under your control at all times.

If you have questions about how your location data is handled, please contact us at support@shramikconnect.in.
