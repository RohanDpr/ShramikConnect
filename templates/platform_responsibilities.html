Platform Responsibilities

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains the role and responsibilities of the ShramikConnect platform. ShramikConnect is designed to help workers and employers discover each other and communicate about job opportunities. The platform provides tools and services that facilitate these connections while maintaining a safe and reliable environment.

1.

Providing a Connection Platform

ShramikConnect provides a digital platform where:

  • Workers can search for job opportunities
  • Employers can find workers for different types of work
  • Users can communicate regarding job requirements

The platform focuses on making it easier for users to discover opportunities and connect with each other.

2.

Maintaining Platform Accessibility

ShramikConnect aims to keep the platform accessible and functional for users.

The platform may perform regular updates and maintenance to improve performance, fix technical issues, and introduce new features.

Temporary service interruptions may occur during these maintenance activities.

3.

Promoting Safe Platform Usage

ShramikConnect encourages safe and responsible use of the platform.

The platform may monitor reported activities and take action when policies are violated in order to protect users from harmful or inappropriate behavior.

4.

Protecting User Information

ShramikConnect makes reasonable efforts to protect user data and account information.

The platform uses appropriate measures to maintain the confidentiality and security of user data.

Users are also responsible for protecting their account credentials.

5.

Reviewing Reported Issues

Users may report problems related to platform usage such as:

  • Misleading job posts
  • Inappropriate behavior
  • Suspicious activities

ShramikConnect may review such reports and take action where necessary according to platform policies.

6.

Improving Platform Features

ShramikConnect may continuously improve the platform to enhance user experience.

This may include:

  • Adding new features
  • Improving job discovery tools
  • Enhancing communication capabilities
  • Updating system performance

These improvements help maintain an effective platform for workers and employers.

7.

Maintaining Neutral Platform Role

ShramikConnect maintains a neutral role between workers and employers.

The platform provides tools for connection and communication but does not participate in job agreements between users.

All work arrangements and agreements are handled directly by the users involved.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect's responsibility is limited to providing a digital platform that connects workers and employers.

The platform does not:

  • Supervise work performed by users
  • Manage payments between users
  • Guarantee job completion or job availability

Users are responsible for managing their own agreements and work arrangements.
