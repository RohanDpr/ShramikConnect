Dispute Resolution Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains how disputes related to the use of the ShramikConnect platform may be handled. ShramikConnect provides a platform that connects workers and employers. Since job agreements and payments occur directly between users, disputes should primarily be resolved between the involved parties.

1.

Direct Resolution Between Users

If a disagreement or conflict arises between a worker and an employer, both parties are encouraged to communicate directly and attempt to resolve the issue.

This may include discussions regarding:

  • Job expectations
  • Work completion
  • Payment agreements
  • Job cancellation or misunderstandings

Direct communication often helps resolve disputes quickly and effectively.

2.

Platform Assistance

ShramikConnect may provide limited assistance when disputes are reported.

The platform may review complaints related to:

  • Misconduct
  • Misleading job postings
  • Abusive behavior between users

However, the platform does not act as a mediator for financial or contractual disputes.

3.

Payment Disputes

ShramikConnect does not process payments between workers and employers.

Because of this:

  • The platform cannot hold or release payments
  • The platform cannot enforce payment agreements
  • Payment conflicts must be resolved directly between the parties involved

Users are encouraged to clearly discuss payment terms before starting work.

4.

Documentation and Communication

In case of disputes, users are encouraged to maintain clear communication and keep records of important details such as:

  • Job descriptions
  • Agreed payment amounts
  • Work timelines
  • Messages exchanged between users

These records may help both parties clarify misunderstandings.

5.

Legal Resolution

If a dispute cannot be resolved through direct communication, users may choose to pursue resolution through appropriate legal channels.

Any legal matters related to the use of the ShramikConnect platform may fall under the jurisdiction of courts located in Buldhana District, Maharashtra, India.

Users are responsible for handling their legal matters independently.

6.

Platform Neutrality

ShramikConnect maintains a neutral role in disputes between workers and employers.

The platform does not guarantee:

  • Job outcomes
  • Work quality
  • Payment fulfillment

Its primary role is to provide a connection platform for users.

7.

Responsible Use of the Platform

Users are encouraged to act responsibly when entering work arrangements through the platform.

Clear communication, transparency, and mutual respect can significantly reduce the likelihood of disputes.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect encourages users to resolve disputes respectfully and responsibly.

The platform's role is limited to connecting workers and employers and does not extend to managing work agreements or enforcing financial arrangements.
