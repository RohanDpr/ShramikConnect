Data Security

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

ShramikConnect takes the security of your personal information seriously. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. This section explains the security practices we follow to keep your information safe.

1.

Encryption

We use industry-standard encryption to protect your data:

  • All data transmitted between your device and our servers is encrypted using SSL/TLS technology (HTTPS). This ensures that your information cannot be intercepted or read by unauthorized parties during transmission.
  • Sensitive information, such as passwords, is hashed and securely stored in our database. This means that even in the unlikely event of a data breach, your actual password would not be exposed.
  • Identity documents (Aadhar, PAN) provided by users are stored with additional security measures to protect this sensitive information.

2.

Secure Hosting Infrastructure

ShramikConnect is hosted on Render, a trusted cloud platform that provides:

  • Secure data centers with physical security measures
  • Network firewalls and intrusion detection systems
  • Regular data backups to prevent data loss
  • High availability and redundancy to ensure platform reliability

Render implements comprehensive security measures that meet industry standards. You can learn more about Render's security practices on their official website.

3.

Access Controls

We strictly limit access to user data within our organization:

  • Only authorized personnel with a legitimate business need can access user data
  • All employee access is protected by strong authentication and logged for audit purposes
  • Regular access reviews are conducted to ensure permissions remain appropriate

We follow the principle of least privilege, meaning employees only have access to the minimum data necessary to perform their job functions.

4.

Authentication and Account Security

We provide security features to help protect your account:

  • OTP (One-Time Password) verification for mobile number authentication
  • Session management to automatically log out inactive users
  • Account lockout mechanisms after multiple failed login attempts to prevent brute force attacks

Your Responsibility

You play an important role in keeping your account secure. Always use a strong, unique password, never share your login credentials with others, and log out of your account when using shared devices.

5.

Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information only as long as necessary:

  • Active accounts: Data is retained while your account is active
  • Inactive accounts: Accounts inactive for 12 months may be deactivated
  • Account deletion: When you request account deletion via email, we delete your account and associated data within 2 days

Some information may be retained longer if required for legal compliance or to resolve disputes, but only to the extent necessary.

6.

Security Monitoring and Incident Response

We continuously monitor our platform for potential security threats:

  • Regular monitoring for suspicious activities and potential security incidents
  • Automated alerts for unusual patterns that may indicate a security breach
  • Established incident response procedures to quickly address any security issues

In the unlikely event of a data breach that affects your personal information, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.

Important Notice

While we implement strong security measures, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We continuously work to maintain and improve our security practices to protect your data.

If you have any questions about the security of your data or suspect any unauthorized activity on your account, please contact us immediately at support@shramikconnect.in.
