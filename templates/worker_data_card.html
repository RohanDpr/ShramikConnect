Worker Data

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains the specific types of personal information we collect from workers on the ShramikConnect platform. We collect only the information necessary to help workers find suitable job opportunities and connect with employers.

1.

Basic Account Information

When workers register on ShramikConnect, we collect essential account information:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number (verified via OTP)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Password (encrypted)

This information is necessary to create and maintain your worker account and to communicate with you about job opportunities.

2.

Profile Information

To help employers find you and understand your capabilities, we collect additional profile details:

  • Location (city/area where you're available for work)
  • Skills and work categories (e.g., plumber, electrician, painter, construction worker, etc.)
  • Work experience (years of experience, previous work history - optional)
  • Preferred working hours and availability
  • Expected wages or payment preferences (optional)

This information helps employers understand your skills and experience, making it easier for them to offer you relevant job opportunities.

3.

Identity Verification Documents

To enhance trust and safety on the platform, we may request identity verification documents:

  • Aadhar card - Optional but recommended for verification. Helps build trust with employers.
  • PAN card - Optional alternative for identity verification.

Secure Storage

When provided, identity documents are stored securely in our database and used only for verification purposes. We take appropriate measures to protect this sensitive information. You can choose not to provide these documents, but verified workers may receive more job opportunities from employers.

Providing identity documents is completely voluntary. However, employers often prefer to hire workers who have completed verification as it increases trust.

4.

Job Application Data

When you apply for jobs or interact with employers, we collect information related to your job-seeking activities:

  • Job application history (jobs you've applied for)
  • Messages exchanged with employers
  • Job start dates and work duration (when applicable)
  • Ratings and feedback received from employers

This information helps us improve your job recommendations and allows employers to see your work history and reliability.

5.

Ratings and Feedback

After job completion, employers may provide ratings and feedback about your work:

  • Star ratings - 1 to 5 stars based on work quality, behavior, punctuality, and professionalism
  • Written feedback - Comments about your work performance

Your ratings and feedback are visible to other employers on the platform. Good ratings can help you get more job opportunities. You cannot delete individual ratings, but you can respond to feedback if needed.

6.

Worker Rights Regarding Data

As a worker on ShramikConnect, you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access - Request a copy of your data anytime
  • Update - Edit your profile information through account settings
  • Delete - Request account deletion via email (processed within 2 days)

For any requests related to your data, please contact us at support@shramikconnect.in.

Important Notice for Workers

The information you provide as a worker helps employers find and hire you. Providing accurate and complete information increases your chances of finding suitable work opportunities.

You are in control of your profile. You can update your information at any time, and you have the right to request deletion of your account when you no longer wish to use ShramikConnect.
