Niraj Nake is a dedicated lecturer and mentor at Government Polytechnic Khamgaon, where he has been teaching computer science for over a decade. His expertise lies in Operating Systems, C, and C++ programming languages.

As a mentor to the ShramikConnect developer team, he has guided them through complex technical challenges, helping them build a robust and scalable platform. His teaching philosophy emphasizes practical learning and problem-solving skills.
