User Conduct Policy

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section describes the standards of behavior expected from all users of the ShramikConnect platform. To maintain a safe and respectful environment for workers and employers, all users must follow the conduct rules outlined below. Failure to comply with these rules may result in account restrictions or suspension.

1.

Respectful Communication

All users must communicate with each other in a respectful and professional manner.

Users must not engage in:

  • Harassment
  • Threats or intimidation
  • Abusive or offensive language
  • Discriminatory remarks

Respectful interaction helps maintain a trustworthy platform for everyone.

2.

Honest Representation

Users must provide truthful and accurate information while using the platform.

This includes:

  • Personal profile information
  • Skills and work experience
  • Job descriptions posted by employers

Misleading or false information can harm other users and may lead to account suspension.

3.

Prohibited Activities

The following activities are strictly prohibited on the platform:

  • Illegal job postings
  • Fraud or scam activities
  • Requesting dangerous or unlawful work
  • Posting misleading job offers
  • Using the platform for non-work related exploitation

Any such activity may result in immediate account suspension.

4.

Fair Interaction Between Users

Workers and employers should treat each other fairly and maintain professional conduct.

Users should not:

  • Exploit workers
  • Force unreasonable work demands
  • Provide misleading instructions regarding work

Maintaining fairness ensures a healthy and reliable environment for all platform users.

5.

Account Usage

Users must only operate one account per individual.

Users must not:

  • Create multiple accounts to bypass restrictions
  • Use someone else's account
  • Allow unauthorized access to their account

Users are responsible for all activity that occurs through their accounts.

6.

Safety and Legal Compliance

All users must follow applicable laws and safety practices while arranging work through the platform.

Users should avoid any work arrangements that may:

  • Violate local laws
  • Create unsafe working conditions
  • Harm individuals or property

ShramikConnect encourages users to prioritize safety in all interactions.

7.

Reporting Misconduct

If a user experiences or observes misconduct on the platform, they are encouraged to report it.

Reports may include:

  • Harassment
  • Fraudulent activities
  • Unsafe job requests

ShramikConnect may review reported cases and take appropriate action.

8.

Consequences of Violations

Violation of the conduct policy may result in actions such as:

  • Removal of job listings
  • Temporary account suspension
  • Permanent account termination in severe cases

The platform reserves the right to take necessary action to protect users and maintain platform integrity.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect aims to create a safe and professional environment where workers and employers can connect.

All users are expected to follow the conduct rules to ensure respectful interactions and trustworthy platform usage.
