Worker Terms

This section explains the responsibilities, expectations, and rules that apply to workers who use the ShramikConnect platform to find job opportunities.

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains the responsibilities, expectations, and rules that apply to workers who use the ShramikConnect platform to find job opportunities. Workers must follow these terms while using the platform to maintain a safe and reliable environment for all users.

1.

Worker Registration

To use ShramikConnect as a worker, users must create a worker profile and provide accurate information.

During registration, workers may be required to provide:

  • Mobile number verification
  • Basic personal details
  • Skill or work category
  • Current location

Workers are responsible for ensuring that the information provided in their profile is correct and updated.

2.

Skill Information

Workers must clearly mention their skills, experience, or type of work they are able to perform.

This helps employers understand the worker's capabilities before offering job opportunities.

ShramikConnect does not guarantee the accuracy of self-declared skills and encourages employers to communicate directly with workers before confirming any work.

3.

Job Applications

Workers may browse available jobs on the platform and apply to jobs that match their skills.

Before applying for a job, workers should:

  • Read the job description carefully
  • Confirm the job location
  • Understand the work requirements

Workers should only apply for jobs they are capable of completing.

4.

Communication with Employers

Once a worker applies for a job, communication may take place directly between the worker and the employer through the platform or other mutually agreed methods.

Workers should maintain professional and respectful communication with employers.

Any abusive, threatening, or inappropriate behavior may result in account suspension.

5.

Payment Negotiation

ShramikConnect does not manage or process payments between workers and employers.

Workers and employers are responsible for negotiating payment terms directly.

Payment methods, timing, and amounts must be agreed upon by both parties independently.

The platform is not responsible for payment disputes.

6.

Work Completion

Workers are expected to complete the work they agree to perform in a timely and responsible manner.

If a worker is unable to complete the work, they should inform the employer as soon as possible.

Failure to complete agreed work without proper communication may affect the worker's reputation on the platform.

7.

Ratings and Feedback

Employers may provide ratings or feedback after a job interaction.

ShramikConnect uses a 5-star rating system to help maintain trust within the platform.

Ratings may reflect:

  • Work quality
  • Reliability
  • Professional behavior
  • Communication

Consistently poor ratings may affect a worker's ability to receive job opportunities.

8.

Worker Responsibility

Workers are responsible for:

  • Performing work safely
  • Following employer instructions related to the job
  • Maintaining professional behavior
  • Ensuring the accuracy of their profile information

Workers must ensure that the work they accept is within their skills and capabilities.

9.

Independent Work Relationship

Workers using ShramikConnect act as independent individuals.

The platform does not create an employment relationship between the worker and ShramikConnect.

ShramikConnect only provides a platform to connect workers with employers.

Workers are responsible for their own agreements with employers.

10.

Platform Role

ShramikConnect only facilitates connections between workers and employers.

The platform does not:

  • Guarantee job availability
  • Guarantee payment
  • Supervise work performed by workers

Workers should communicate clearly with employers before accepting any job opportunity.
