Employer Terms

This section explains the responsibilities, expectations, and rules for employers who use the ShramikConnect platform to connect with workers.

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains the responsibilities, expectations, and rules for employers who use the ShramikConnect platform to connect with workers. Employers must follow these guidelines to maintain a fair, safe, and transparent environment for all users of the platform.

1.

Employer Registration

To use ShramikConnect as an employer, users must create an employer profile and provide accurate information.

During registration, employers may be required to provide:

  • Mobile number verification
  • Basic personal details
  • Job location information

Employers must ensure that the information provided in their account is correct and up to date.

2.

Job Posting Requirements

Employers can post job opportunities on the platform to find suitable workers.

When posting a job, employers should clearly mention:

  • Type of work
  • Job location
  • Expected work duration
  • Basic work requirements

Providing clear and accurate job descriptions helps workers understand the nature of the job before applying.

3.

Honest Job Information

Employers must ensure that all job postings are accurate and truthful.

Job posts must not contain:

  • Misleading information
  • Fake job opportunities
  • Illegal work requests

If false or misleading job details are reported, the platform may remove the listing or suspend the employer account.

4.

Communication with Workers

Employers should communicate clearly and respectfully with workers.

Employers must provide workers with clear instructions about the work, location, and expectations before the job begins.

Harassment, abusive language, or inappropriate behavior toward workers may result in account suspension.

5.

Payment Agreement

ShramikConnect does not process or control payments between employers and workers.

Employers and workers must negotiate payment terms directly with each other.

Employers are responsible for paying workers according to the agreed terms once the work is completed.

The platform is not responsible for payment disputes between users.

6.

Job Cancellation

If an employer needs to cancel a job after workers have applied, the employer should communicate directly with the workers involved.

ShramikConnect does not manage cancellations or compensation arrangements.

Employers and workers must resolve such situations through direct communication.

7.

Fair Treatment of Workers

Employers must treat workers fairly and respectfully.

Employers should not:

  • Discriminate against workers
  • Exploit workers
  • Request unsafe or illegal work

Maintaining respectful behavior helps build a trustworthy community on the platform.

8.

Independent Hiring Relationship

Workers found through ShramikConnect are independent individuals.

Using the platform does not create an employment relationship between the employer and ShramikConnect.

Any work agreement exists only between the employer and the worker.

ShramikConnect acts only as a platform connecting both parties.

9.

Platform Limitations

ShramikConnect provides tools to help employers find workers but does not guarantee:

  • Worker availability
  • Worker performance
  • Successful job completion

Employers should communicate directly with workers before confirming any work arrangements.

10.

Responsibility of Employers

Employers are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate job details
  • Communicating clearly with workers
  • Paying workers as agreed
  • Ensuring safe working conditions

Failure to follow these responsibilities may result in account restrictions.
