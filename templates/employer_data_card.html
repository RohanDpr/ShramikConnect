Employer Data

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains the specific types of personal information we collect from employers on the ShramikConnect platform. We collect only the information necessary to help employers find suitable workers and post job opportunities effectively.

1.

Basic Account Information

When employers register on ShramikConnect, we collect essential account information:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number (verified via OTP)
  • Email address (optional but recommended)
  • Password (encrypted)

This information is necessary to create and maintain your employer account and to communicate with workers about job opportunities.

2.

Business Information

To help workers understand who they might be working for, we collect business-related information:

  • Business name - Optional. You can operate as an individual or provide your business/organization name.
  • Location - City/area where you need workers (can be your business location or job site location).
  • Work category - Types of workers you typically hire (construction, plumbing, electrical, domestic help, etc.).

Note: Business registration documents (GST, company registration) are NOT required to use ShramikConnect. Individuals can hire workers without any business registration.

3.

Job Posting Data

When you post jobs on the platform, we collect and store the following information:

  • Job title and description - Details about the work to be performed
  • Required skills - Skills needed for the job
  • Work location - Where the job will take place
  • Duration and timing - Expected work duration, start date, working hours
  • Payment offered - Wages or payment amount (optional - can be discussed with workers)

Job postings are visible to workers on the platform. Clear and accurate job descriptions help attract suitable workers.

4.

Hiring and Communication Data

When you interact with workers and hire them, we collect information related to these activities:

  • Job posting history - All jobs you have posted on the platform
  • Applicant information - Workers who have applied to your jobs
  • Messages exchanged - Communication with workers about job details
  • Hiring decisions - Which workers you selected for jobs

This information helps you track your hiring activities and manage your interactions with workers.

5.

Ratings and Feedback Given

After job completion, employers may provide ratings and feedback about workers:

  • Star ratings - 1 to 5 stars based on work quality, behavior, punctuality, and professionalism
  • Written feedback - Comments about the worker's performance

Impact of Your Ratings

The ratings and feedback you provide help other employers make informed decisions when hiring workers. Honest and fair ratings contribute to a trustworthy community on ShramikConnect.

6.

Employer Rights Regarding Data

As an employer on ShramikConnect, you have the following rights regarding your data:

  • Access - Request a copy of your data anytime
  • Update - Edit your profile information through account settings
  • Delete - Request account deletion via email (processed within 2 days)

Data Requests

For any requests related to your data, please contact us at support@shramikconnect.in

Important Notice for Employers

The information you provide as an employer helps workers find suitable job opportunities. Providing accurate job details increases your chances of finding the right workers for your needs.

No business registration required: You can hire workers as an individual without any business registration, GST, or company documents. ShramikConnect is designed to be accessible to all employers, from individuals to large organizations.
