Data Retention

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains how long ShramikConnect retains your personal information, the criteria we use to determine retention periods, and what happens to your data when you delete your account. We retain data only as long as necessary to provide our services and fulfill the purposes described in this privacy policy.

1.

General Retention Principle

ShramikConnect retains your personal information only for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide you with access to our platform and services
  • Fulfill the purposes for which the information was collected
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements

Once the retention period expires, your personal information is securely deleted or anonymized so that it can no longer be associated with you.

2.

Active Account Data Retention

While your account is active, we retain your personal information to provide you with uninterrupted access to our services:

Profile Information

Retained for the entire duration your account remains active

Active Account

Activity History

Job applications, messages, and interactions are retained while your account is active

Active Account

Ratings and Feedback

Ratings you give and receive are retained as part of your profile history

Active Account

3.

Inactive Account Deactivation

To maintain data accuracy and reduce security risks, we have an inactive account policy:

12-Month Inactivity Period

Accounts that remain inactive for 12 consecutive months may be automatically deactivated. An account is considered inactive if the user has not logged in or performed any platform activity during this period.

Before deactivation, we may attempt to notify you using the contact information associated with your account. After deactivation, you can reactivate your account by simply logging in again.

4.

Account Deletion Process

When you request account deletion, we follow this process:

1

User Request

You send an email to support@shramikconnect.in requesting account deletion

2

Verification

We verify your identity to ensure the request is legitimate

3

Processing Period

Within 2 days of receiving and verifying your request, we initiate the deletion process

4

Data Removal

Your account and associated personal information are permanently removed from our active systems

Note: Some information may be retained longer if required for legal compliance, fraud prevention, or to resolve disputes, but only to the extent necessary and for the minimum period required by law.

6.

Legal and Compliance Retention

In certain circumstances, we may need to retain information for longer periods:

  • Legal Obligations: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, or court orders
  • Fraud Prevention: To investigate and prevent fraudulent activities
  • Dispute Resolution: To resolve ongoing disputes or enforce our agreements
  • Tax and Accounting: To meet financial record-keeping requirements

When information is retained for these purposes, it is accessed only as necessary and stored securely.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect is committed to retaining your personal information only as long as necessary. You have control over your data through account deletion requests, which are processed within 2 days.

If you have questions about our data retention practices or need assistance with account deletion, please contact us at support@shramikconnect.in.
