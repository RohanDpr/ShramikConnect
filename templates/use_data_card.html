How We Use Data

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains how ShramikConnect uses the personal information we collect from users. We use your data only for legitimate purposes related to providing and improving our platform services, and we never sell your personal information to third parties.

1.

Account Management

We use your account information to:

  • Create and maintain your user account
  • Authenticate your identity when you log in
  • Protect your account from unauthorized access
  • Send important account-related notifications

These uses are essential for providing you with access to our platform and ensuring the security of your account.

2.

Platform Functionality

We use your profile and activity data to enable core platform features:

  • Help workers discover relevant job opportunities
  • Help employers find workers with matching skills
  • Facilitate communication between workers and employers
  • Display ratings and feedback to build trust

This data is essential for the matching and connection services that ShramikConnect provides.

3.

Service Improvement

We analyze usage patterns and technical data to improve our platform:

  • Understand how users interact with the platform
  • Identify and fix technical issues
  • Optimize platform performance and user experience
  • Develop new features based on user needs

This helps us make ShramikConnect more useful and user-friendly for everyone.

4.

Communication

We use your contact information to communicate with you about:

  • Job applications and responses from employers
  • Messages from workers or employers
  • Responses to your support inquiries
  • Important updates about platform policies or security

We do not send marketing emails or promotional messages without your consent. Currently, we do not engage in marketing communications.

5.

Trust and Safety

We use data to maintain a safe and trustworthy platform:

  • Verify user identities when needed
  • Detect and prevent fraudulent or suspicious activity
  • Investigate reported violations of platform policies
  • Take appropriate action against misconduct

These measures help protect all users and maintain the integrity of our community.

6.

Legal Compliance

We may use or disclose your information when required by law:

  • Comply with legal obligations and court orders
  • Respond to requests from law enforcement or government authorities
  • Establish, exercise, or defend legal claims

We only share information in response to valid legal requests, and we notify users when permitted by law.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect uses your personal information only for the purposes described in this policy. We do not sell your data to third parties, and we do not use your information for advertising or marketing without your explicit consent.

If our data usage practices change in the future, we will update this policy and notify users as required by applicable law.
