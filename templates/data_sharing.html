Third-Party Data Sharing

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

ShramikConnect values your privacy and does not sell your personal information to third parties. However, there are limited circumstances where we may share data with trusted third-party service providers to operate and improve our platform. This section explains when and how we share information with third parties.

1.

We Do Not Sell Your Data

ShramikConnect does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Your data is only used to provide and improve our platform services as described in this privacy policy.

2.

Service Providers and Infrastructure

We engage trusted third-party service providers to help us operate our platform. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your data and only use it for the specific services they provide to us.

Hosting Services

  • Render - Our platform is hosted on Render's cloud infrastructure. Render may have access to data stored on their servers as necessary to provide hosting services, but they are contractually bound to maintain confidentiality and security.

Analytics and Monitoring

  • We may use analytics tools to understand how users interact with our platform. These tools collect anonymous usage data and do not identify individual users.

All service providers are carefully vetted and must agree to strict data protection requirements before accessing any user information.

3.

YouTube and Embedded Content

Our platform may display embedded YouTube videos to help users understand how ShramikConnect works. When you watch these videos:

  • YouTube may collect data about your interaction with the video, including viewing history and device information.
  • This data collection is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by ShramikConnect's privacy policy.
  • We do not control what information YouTube collects or how they use it.

You can review Google's privacy policy to understand how YouTube handles your data: https://policies.google.com/privacy

4.

Legal Requirements and Law Enforcement

We may disclose your personal information if required by law or in response to valid legal requests, such as:

  • Court orders or legal processes
  • Requests from law enforcement or government authorities
  • Compliance with applicable laws and regulations

When permitted by law, we will make reasonable efforts to notify users of such requests before disclosing their information.

5.

Protection of Platform Rights

We may share information when necessary to:

  • Protect the rights, property, or safety of ShramikConnect, our users, or others
  • Investigate and prevent fraud, security issues, or technical problems
  • Enforce our Terms and Conditions or other platform policies

In such cases, we will only share information to the extent reasonably necessary to address the situation.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect does not sell your personal information to third parties. Any sharing of data is limited to specific, legitimate purposes as described in this policy, and we require all third parties to maintain appropriate security and confidentiality measures.

If you have questions about how your data is shared, please contact us at support@shramikconnect.in.
