Liability and Disclaimer

Last Updated: March 2026

Overview

This section explains the limitations of responsibility for the ShramikConnect platform. ShramikConnect acts only as a digital platform that connects workers and employers. The platform does not directly participate in job agreements or financial transactions between users. By using the platform, users acknowledge and accept the limitations described in this section.

1.

Platform Role

ShramikConnect operates as a technology platform that allows workers and employers to connect with each other.

The platform provides tools that help users:

  • Discover job opportunities
  • Connect with workers or employers
  • Communicate regarding work

ShramikConnect does not act as an employer, contractor, or agent for any user.

2.

No Employment Relationship

Using ShramikConnect does not create an employment relationship between:

  • Workers and the platform
  • Employers and the platform

Any work agreement exists only between the worker and the employer.

ShramikConnect is not involved in managing or supervising work.

3.

Payment Responsibility

ShramikConnect does not process or manage payments between users.

Workers and employers negotiate payment terms directly and handle payments independently.

The platform is not responsible for:

  • Payment delays
  • Payment disputes
  • Non-payment issues

Users must resolve payment matters directly with each other.

4.

Job Quality and Performance

ShramikConnect does not guarantee the quality, reliability, or performance of workers.

Similarly, the platform does not guarantee that employers will provide suitable work conditions.

Users are encouraged to communicate clearly and verify job details before accepting work.

5.

User Conduct Responsibility

Users are responsible for their own actions while using the platform.

ShramikConnect is not responsible for:

  • Misconduct by workers or employers
  • False information provided by users
  • Any damages caused during work arrangements

If disputes arise between users, they must be resolved between the involved parties.

6.

Platform Availability

While ShramikConnect aims to provide reliable service, the platform does not guarantee uninterrupted availability.

Temporary interruptions may occur due to:

  • Maintenance updates
  • Technical issues
  • Internet connectivity problems

ShramikConnect is not liable for losses resulting from such interruptions.

7.

Third-Party Communication

Users may communicate outside the platform after connecting.

ShramikConnect does not control or monitor external communication channels.

Therefore, the platform is not responsible for any agreements, misunderstandings, or issues that arise from such interactions.

8.

Limitation of Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, ShramikConnect shall not be liable for:

  • Financial losses
  • Work-related damages
  • Contract disputes between users
  • Any indirect or consequential damages

Users agree that they use the platform at their own discretion and responsibility.

9.

User Responsibility

By using the platform, users agree that they are responsible for:

  • Verifying job details
  • Negotiating payment terms
  • Ensuring safe working arrangements
  • Following applicable laws

ShramikConnect only facilitates connections and does not participate in user agreements.

Important Notice

ShramikConnect provides a platform to help workers and employers find each other.

The platform does not:

  • Guarantee job completion
  • Guarantee payment
  • Supervise work performed by users

All agreements and responsibilities remain between the worker and the employer.
